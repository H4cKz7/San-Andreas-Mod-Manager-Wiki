## Installer or portable

| Version | Updates | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| Installer | Download and restart from the update button | Most people |
| Portable | Swap the new build in by hand | USB sticks, locked-down PCs |

## Choose your GTA folder

1. Click **Scan GTA folder**.
2. Pick the folder that contains `gta_sa.exe`.
3. Wait for the mod list to appear.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Keep a backup of your game folder before you change anything the first time.
