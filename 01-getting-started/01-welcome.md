SA Mod Manager is a desktop app for managing GTA San Andreas mods that run through Mod Loader. It lists your mods, lets you group them into profiles and edits their config files, without digging through folders.

## Where to go next

- New here? Read [[Installing the app]] first.
- Adding a mod? See [[Installing a mod]].
- Something broke? Start with [[Mod does not load]].
