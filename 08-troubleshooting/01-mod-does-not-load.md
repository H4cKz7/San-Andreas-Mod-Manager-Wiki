---
title: Mod does not load
summary: A checklist for a mod that is installed but does nothing in game.
tags: troubleshooting, modloader, logs
---

## Check in this order

1. The mod is enabled in the **current** profile.
2. `modloader.log` shows no error for that mod. Open it from **Logs**.
3. Required ASI plugins are present. Run the version and essentials checker.

> [!TIP]
> Still stuck? Export the mod list and attach it when you ask for help.
