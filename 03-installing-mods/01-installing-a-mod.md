## Steps

1. Click **Install mod** and choose the archive, or drop it on the window.
2. Review the plan the installer shows.
3. Confirm. The mod appears in the list.

Password-protected archives ask for the password first.

> [!WARNING]
> Mods that replace game files directly are not handled by Mod Loader. Check the mod's readme.

See also [[Profiles]] and [[Mod does not load]].
